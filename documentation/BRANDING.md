# Leadpulse Branding Guidelines

This document outlines the visual and verbal identity for Leadpulse (formerly PulseSignal).

## 1. Naming & Identity

*   **Platform Name**: Leadpulse
*   **Company Context**: The application is often referred to internally as "The Signal Engine" or "Intelligence Hub".
*   **User Persona**: Sales representatives, Account Executives, and Growth Managers.

## 2. Banned Terms (Legacy)

To maintain a consistent brand, the following terms **MUST NOT** be used in user-facing UI or documentation:

*   **AI**: Use "Leadpulse", "Intelligence", or "Automation".
*   **Gemini / Gemini Flash**: Refer to "Leadpulse Core" or simply "Leadpulse".
*   **SalesPulse**: The legacy project name. Always use "Leadpulse".
*   **Flash**: Avoid referring to the underlying model speed; focus on the result (Real-time).

## 3. Product Tone

*   **Strategic & Sharp**: Leadpulse provides "Strategic Dossiers" and "Market Intensity" maps.
*   **Actionable**: Every signal should lead to an action (e.g., "Push to CRM", "Email Briefing").
*   **Real-time**: Emphasize that signals are from the "Live Web" or "Live Hunt".

## 4. Visual Identity

*   **Primary Color**: Indigo (#6C5DD3) - Used for primary actions, badges, and progress.
*   **Secondary Color**: Zinc/Slate - Used for secondary text and background layers.
*   **Icons**: Consistent use of `lucide-react` (Radio, Radar, Target, Sparkles).
